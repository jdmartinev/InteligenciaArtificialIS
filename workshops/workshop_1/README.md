**Description:**  
This workshop integrates the core topics of the first module of the course, including problem formulation, state-space search, planning, and scheduling. Students will work with both uninformed and informed search strategies, and will explore metaheuristic approaches for solving optimization problems.  

Through hands-on experimentation, students will analyze algorithm behavior, compare solution quality and computational cost, and reflect on the trade-offs between exact and heuristic methods.  

The objective is to consolidate conceptual understanding while developing the practical skills required to model problems and implement solution strategies in real scenarios.
